# v11: v10 plus what v12/v13 had, and fixes from the ladder losses

**v11 beat v10 71–45 (61%) over 116 games** (41–27 on the 17 rebuilt ladder maps, 30–18 on maps_worldgen), no map data on either side, no timeouts, peak 58–63M points per turn. Its four changes from v10, each a switch in `bots/v11/params.hpp`:

- `SPLIT_RELAXED`: a dragon splits when one half is safe and the child has an exit.
- `ENDGAME_HORIZON`: room for the moves left in the game is enough to count as safe.
- `QUEEN_KEEP_LENGTH`: the queen escape-splits only when no move leaves her 3 tiles of room.
- `QUEEN_SEALED_FALLBACK`: dragons that cannot reach our queen feed the biggest ally in view instead.

Tested and left off: `EVAL_MAX_DEPTH` (v13's CPU cap), reckless dives, and long-range exploration (`EXPLORE_FAR_W`).

v11 is in `bots/v11`. It starts from v10 (commit `709224d`, fs2's swarm with v9's queen, no map data; see `docs/v10.md`). Every change is behind a switch in `bots/v11/params.hpp` and is tested head to head against a frozen v10 (`results/tmp_bots/v10_ref`) with `tools/ab_v10.sh`.

## What v12 and v13 had that v10 lacks

Ladder submission v12 is v9 at `ddb803e`; v13 ("v9.1") is `950668a`. v10 was merged from v9 at `b7daa98`, which already contains all of v12 and the fixes that followed it (split timing, long queens, queen food). v10 also drops map data, as v13 does.

The only v13 feature not in v10 is the CPU guard on each move's search:

- **`EVAL_MAX_DEPTH`** = 30 (ported). Each candidate move's search of the room it can reach stops 30 steps out, or at the dragon's length plus 8 if that is longer, so it still finds its own tail. Pearls that far away are worth almost nothing (0.85^30 < 1%). With it, v13 peaked at 31M points per turn on the 64×64 maps; v10 peaks at about 60M with its `MULTI_VISIT_BUDGET`.
- v13's other guard (2-step paths on maps over 2,048 tiles) is not ported. v10's path budget does the same job.

## Fixes from the v14 (v10) ladder losses

v10 went 34–27 in its first 61 ladder games, then lost 4–1 to Deer Park and 0–5 to we love gru. In 7 of those 9 losses their queen died first (rounds 17–390), and we lost because ours died too.

- **Our queen splits herself to death.** In escape splits she gave away nearly her whole body and kept 2 segments: 13 → 2 (Devil, round 302), 14 → 2 (Tower Defense, 329), 37 → 2 (Around UNSW, 484), and four times on Maze. Then she died. On Portals, 26 → 2 at round 493 and 31 → 2 at 481 cost games that her survival at any length would have won. The cause: a split-off piece of 2 counts as "safe" with 8 tiles of room, but her whole body needs 2×length+4 tiles, rarely found mid-game. Two fixes:
  - `ENDGAME_HORIZON`: room for the moves left in the game (plus 2) is enough to be safe. Neutral in self-play (34–34); kept for the ladder case.
  - `QUEEN_KEEP_LENGTH`: the queen escape-splits only when no move leaves her 3 tiles of room.
- **Schooltime's sealed queens.** Against Deer Park both queens lived at length 3, so the longest dragon decided: their 59 against our 22, with 44 of our dragons still alive at round 500 and none able to reach our queen. `QUEEN_SEALED_FALLBACK`: from round 400, a dragon with no known path to our queen feeds the biggest ally it sees instead.
- **We are out-eaten on unseen maps** (not fixed yet). By round 300: Prisoners Dilemma 37 v 170, weakhold 109 v 360, Tower Defense 59 v 290, Devil 314 v 821, Australia 193 v 780. On Australia our 9 starting dragons ate 25 pearls in 100 rounds against 157. The pearls sit in two rich bands at the left and right edges (y 24–40, joined across the wrap), 10–20 tiles from our spawn. Our swarm roamed the barren middle for 100 rounds and never found them. Unseen tiles get a flat prior, and a pearl's pull drops to 0.8 per step (about 3% at 15 steps), so nothing draws dragons towards a large unexplored area. Exploration is the next thing to fix: `EXPLORE_FAR_W` (off by default) makes each small dragon head for the nearest never-seen tile that it reaches before any ally. It applies until round 300, on maps of 900+ tiles; a tile whose mirror image is seen counts as seen.
- **weakhold:** our dragons dive into rich dead-end pockets (42 pearls in one) and escape by splitting, leaving the 2-long head to die (22 wall deaths by round 120). Deer Park sacrifices dragons too (124 wall deaths a game there), but its swarm keeps growing: 14 v 4 dragons at round 100. With only two starting dragons each, our growth cannot absorb the losses.

None of these runs had a timeout error (two tests ran at once). Peak points per turn stayed at 56–63M.

The depth cap is switched off in v11 (`EVAL_MAX_DEPTH = 0`): it did not help v10, whose path budget already caps CPU. The candidates are v11 with the depth cap off, so each differs from v10 only in what it tests.
