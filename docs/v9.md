# v9: the queen bot

v9 is v4.46 rebuilt for the rules of 1 October 2026 ("Slay Queen"). It is in `bots/v9`; every new behaviour is a switch in `bots/v9/params.hpp` under `Queens`.

## The rules that changed

- Dragons 0 and 1 are the queens, one per team. At round 500 the longer queen wins, and a dead queen counts as length 0. Ties go to the longest dragon, then to total length. Wiping out the other team still wins at once.
- A dragon of length L moves its first ceil(L/4) steps each turn for free, with L fixed when the move starts. Each further step costs a segment. A length-L enemy can therefore reach ceil(L/4) + L − 2 tiles in one turn if it sprints.

v4.46 knew nothing of queens. Its king was the longest dragon, and its queen split down to length 2 and often fed someone else, then died.

## What v9 does

**The queen is the king.**
- Feeding always goes to our queen (`QUEEN_KING`). A queen record outranks any longer dragon in the sonar KING relay.
- The queen never feeds, never trades heads, and never dives into a dead end.
- If no word of the queen arrives for 30 rounds, the old longest-dragon king takes over.

**The queen splits only early.**
- She swarm-splits like any other dragon until round 60, then only grows (`QUEEN_SPLIT_UNTIL`). This beat never splitting 28–14.
- When she is boxed in, she keeps all of herself while she has room to move.
- If she must split, she keeps the head and values a split-off child at 0.25 per segment.

**The queen avoids enemy reach.**
- Every tile gets the chance an enemy head can reach it before her next turn (`computeQueenThreat`). A tile within reach of the enemy's visible length counts as certain. An enemy whose tail is out of view may be longer, and each extra segment it would need halves the chance. Enemies seen in the last 3 rounds count at half weight from where they were seen.
- The cost is 25,000 times that chance, which is more than being boxed in (20,000).
- Smaller costs keep her away from enemy heads, away from the edge of her view, and out of corridors (fewer than 16 tiles within 3 steps).

**Moves of several steps** (`MULTI_STEP`).
- Dragons of length 5 or more (2+ free steps) also consider every path of up to 3 steps through tiles in view, never turning straight back.
- Each path is simulated step by step (pearls, tail, collisions) and scored from where it ends.
- A long queen grows faster this way and can outrun small hunters.

**Enemy reach** (`NEW_SPRINT`, `OWN_BODY_OPEN`).
- Threat and strike reach use the new sprint rule.
- Enemy reach now passes through our own body. v4.46 treated our body as a wall, so the tile we moved onto, often one our tail was leaving, could look safe with an enemy next to it.

**Hunting.**
- Any dragon except our queen trades heads with the enemy queen. It counts as a gain of 10 segments, and sprints of up to 8 steps are planned for it.

**Feeding.**
- From round 400 a dragon that reaches the queen dies for her at once (`FEED_ROUND`). v4.46 gathered everyone around the king from 400 but fed only from 440, and the crowd boxed the queen in.

## Tested and left off

All of these were played head to head against v9 itself: 7 ladder maps × 3 seeds × both sides = 42 games per test, judge sandbox.

| Change | Result | Kept |
|---|---:|---|
| Queen never splits → splits until round 60 | 28–14 | yes |
| Queen splits until 30 | 19–23 | no |
| Queen splits until 120 | 23–19 | no (noise) |
| Budding: a queen of 8+ sheds a 4-long child (`QUEEN_BUD_LEN`) | 21–21 | no |
| Guards trade with enemy heads near our queen (`QUEEN_GUARD`) | 21–21 | no |
| Enemy-queen sightings over sonar + hunters pulled to them (`QUEEN_HUNT_APPROACH`, `QUEEN_SHARE_AGE`) | 23–19 | no (noise) |
| Split until 120 + hunting pull | 22–20 | no |
| Two-round look-ahead: avoid tiles with no escape next round (`QUEEN_CORNERED` 8,000 / 20,000) | 19–23 / 19–23 | no |
| Hunting pull + guards together (on the never-split version) | 18–24 | no |

A 23–19 result over 42 games is within noise. A change needs a clear margin, or a confirmation on fresh seeds, before it goes in.

## Known weaknesses

- **The queen still dies in most games.** In self-play she is alive at round 500 in roughly 6–13 of 42 games, and in 2 of 96 without map data on generated maps. Most deaths are head-on kills by 2–4-long dragons in the mid game, often when no safe tile is left. This is the main thing to improve.
- **Doomed spawns.** On Autarky, Dilemma and Slithery Fight both queens die by round 5: they spawn facing a dead-end pocket they cannot turn around in. The organisers say new maps will move queen spawns to safer spots, and these three maps are left out of the tests.
- **CPU.** The peak is about 55M points per turn (100M is the limit), up from 19M in v4.46, because of multi-step paths.

## How to test

Always test a change head to head against the current v9, never against other bots: `results/tmp_bots/v9_ref` is a frozen copy (local only, as results/ is not in git; recreate it with `cp -r bots/v9 results/tmp_bots/v9_ref` and refresh it whenever a change is accepted).

```bash
./tools/ab.sh <name> <candidate dir>          # 42 games, map data on
./tools/ab_nomap.sh <name> <candidate dir>    # 48 games, no map data, maps_worldgen
```

`tools/tourney.py` now drops stale sandbox builds. The toolkit's wasm cache keys on .c/.cpp files only, so an edit to `params.hpp` alone used to rerun the old binary.

Results and replays are in `results/v9/`. `tools/queen_stats.cjs` summarises both queens' fates over a folder of replays, `tools/queen_kills.cjs` lists who killed our queen head-on and from where, and `tools/queen_turns.cjs` prints one dragon's last rounds with the enemies around it. Replay a game with `DEBUG = true` in a copy of the bot to see every move's score in the replay log.
