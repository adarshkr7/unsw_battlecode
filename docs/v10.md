# v10: fs2's swarm with v9's queen, no map data

v10 is in `bots/v10`. It is a three-way merge: base v4.46, one side fs2 (the fast-swarm bot), the other side v9 (the queen bot, commit b7daa98). Every feature from both is in, behind the same switches as before (`bots/v10/params.hpp`).

## No map data

v10 carries no map data. `maps_data.hpp`, the map matching (`identifyMap`, `mapMatchesView`, `adoptSpawnBody`) and the `USE_MAP_DATA`, `USE_SPAWN_DATA` and `SPAWN_MISMATCH_OK` params are gone. Every map is played as an unseen one, learned only from what our dragons see and tell each other over sonar. This is how fs2n and v9 already played with `USE_MAP_DATA = false`.

## What comes from where

**From fs2** (portals, exploration, trades):
- Portal pairs are shared over sonar (PORTAL records). With the symmetry known, a known pair also gives its mirror pair.
- Dragons of length 3 or less may go through a portal whose far end nobody knows yet.
- Until round 300, on maps of 900+ tiles, a move earns 15 points per never-seen tile it brings into view (`EXPLORE_*`).
- An even head trade is taken only where our heads near the target outnumber theirs (`TRADE_DENSITY`). A tile an even or shorter enemy can reach costs at least 150 where their heads near it outnumber ours (`THREAT_DENSITY_PENALTY`).
- The anchored king (`ANCHOR_KING`): on maps of 1500+ tiles, from round 150 (2000+ tiles) or 200, one king is fed by small dragons at the dragon cap. It is leashed to where our team started (HOME record) and keeps the last dragon slot for its escape splits. Other dragons keep out of a 3-step ring round it.

**From v9** (the queen):
- The queen is the king. She never feeds, never trades heads, never dives, and swarm-splits only until round 60 and while 8 or shorter.
- She avoids tiles an enemy head can probably reach (`computeQueenThreat`), the edge of her view, corridors and enemy heads.
- Free steps: dragons of 5+ also weigh every path of up to 3 steps.
- Enemy reach follows the new sprint rule and passes through our own body.
- Any dragon but our queen trades heads with the enemy queen.
- Feeding from round 400. Other dragons leave pearls within 3 steps of our queen.
- `SPLIT_CHILD_EARLY`: a split-off child's tail frees before the parent's next move.

## Where the two had to be joined

- **The anchored king is our queen.** fs2 elected the anchored king by length (8+, a challenger needs 3 more). In v10 our queen's record outranks any other at any length, she always claims the crown, and no other dragon can claim it over a valid queen. Only when no word of her arrives for 12 rounds (`ANCHOR_STALE`), or she is seen to be gone, can a dragon of 8+ become king. So from round 150–200 on big maps, small dragons at the cap feed the queen, as v9 did only from round 400.
- **A king seen to be gone comes back with a newer record.** fs2 marked a king dead for 30 rounds when a fresh record put it next to us and we could not see it. That also ignored every later record from it. Now only records no newer than the round of the mark are ignored, so a queen that moved away fast (free steps) or through a portal is not lost for 30 rounds.
- **The queen's dragon slot.** fs2 reserved the last slot under the cap for the king's escape splits. Our queen may also use it while she is not the king.
- **No blind portals for the queen.** fs2's small dragons and portal scouts go through unknown portals; the queen never does.
- **Sonar types.** The message type has 3 bits and fs2 uses all seven values. v9's ENEMY_QUEEN record (off by default, `QUEEN_SHARE_AGE = 0`) now travels as a PREY record carrying the enemy queen's id.
- **Trade value.** `tradeGain` takes both fs2's target tile (for the density rule) and v9's enemy-queen flag. The enemy queen is worth a trade whatever the density.

## CPU

v9's multi-step paths made the cost per turn grow with dragon length and map size. Every path runs its own search of all the room it can reach. On the 64×64 Around UNSW, a dragon of 9 or more (3 free steps, up to 52 moves scored) averaged 48M points per turn and peaked at 96.7M, against a limit of 100M. Dragons of 2–4 averaged 13M. Every other test map stayed under 61M.

`MULTI_VISIT_BUDGET` (80,000 tiles searched) caps it. Each turn v10 takes the largest search a single step needed as the cost of one path. If scoring every path would exceed the budget, it scores every 2-step path and then extends only the best of them to 3 steps until the budget is spent. Below the budget nothing changes: same paths, same order, same games.

Measured on the same game (Around UNSW, seed 5001, per-turn points from the judge sandbox):

| Length | Before: mean / max | After: mean / max |
|---|---:|---:|
| 2–4 | 13.0M / 19.5M | 12.9M / 19.5M |
| 5–8 | 21.3M / 36.6M | 21.1M / 37.1M |
| 9–12 | 48.2M / 93.2M | 36.5M / 58.9M |
| 13+ | 47.5M / 82.7M | 34.8M / 59.6M |

Two switches were added for experiments. Their defaults leave v10's play unchanged:
- `QUEEN_EXPLORE` (default true): false means the queen gets no exploration bonus.
- `QUEEN_PORTAL_PENALTY` (default 0): a cost for any queen move through a portal, known or unknown, including one step of a multi-step path.

## How to test

Test every change head to head against a frozen copy of v10, never with map data:

```bash
cp -r bots/v10 results/tmp_bots/v10_ref    # the reference; refresh it when a change is accepted
tools/ab_v10.sh <name> <candidate dir> [reference dir] [ladder|worldgen|both]
```

`ladder` plays the maps in `maps_ladder/` (rebuilt from ladder replays; set `LADDER=<dir>` for another set), `worldgen` the 12 generated maps in `maps_worldgen/`. Each plays 2 seeds with both sides. A reference with a `USE_MAP_DATA` switch, such as an older v9, is played with it off. Results and replays go to `results/v10/`, which is not in git.

Check every summary for an `errors ... ran out of time` line. The toolkit has a wall-clock limit per turn, and an overloaded machine makes bots time out. Keep to 2–3 tests at once, at normal priority.

The tests above used `results/tmp_bots/v9_b7d` (v9 at b7daa98, with the ladder maps as committed there) and `results/tmp_bots/v91` (950668a, with its 17 maps).
